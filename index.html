About

Andover Born,
Built to Compete

Carter Fandre grew up in Andover, Minnesota — a kid shaped by competition, family, and a deep love of sport. A graduate of Andover High School, home of the Huskies, Carter brought the same drive on the basketball court and soccer field that defines the Fandre family.

Whether running the floor in AAU tournaments or suiting up in black and gold for the Huskies, Carter was a versatile multi-sport athlete — a rare combination of skill sets that translates far beyond the field of play.

Today, Carter channels that competitive edge into his professional career at Federated Insurance in Owatonna — one of the most respected insurance companies in the country, known for its A+ (Superior) AM Best rating and deep Minnesota roots dating back to 1904.

Career

Federated Insurance

Carter is now part of the team at Federated Insurance — headquartered in Owatonna, MN, and one of the country's premier business insurance providers. Founded in 1904, Federated has grown from a small Midwest mutual into a national carrier operating in 48 states, with an A+ (Superior) AM Best rating.

Known for its deep commitment to its clients — primarily small and mid-size businesses — Federated is the kind of company that rewards people who show up, work hard, and care about the people they serve. Sounds like a good fit.
